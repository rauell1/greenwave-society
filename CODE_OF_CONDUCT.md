# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

Everyone who takes part in this project, as a maintainer, contributor or user, deserves a respectful, harassment-free experience, whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind, patient and constructive.
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologise to those affected and learn from them.
- Protect other people's privacy and personal data.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Insults, trolling, derogatory comments and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment, threats or intimidation.
- Publishing someone else's private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions and chats) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the repository owner (GitHub: rauell1). Every report is reviewed promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy is respected.

## Enforcement

The owner decides on the response, in proportion to the behaviour: a private warning, a public warning, a temporary ban from project spaces, or a permanent ban for serious or repeated violations.
