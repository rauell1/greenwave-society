The films

Three cuts, one story.

The Wave

30 s brand anthem. One gold seed lands and draws a line; the line becomes 500 young people, then 10,000 trees, then the lights of 25 communities, and finally folds into the Greenwave emblem.

Six Ways In

20 s programme reel. One beat per programme, from The 50 Percent to Urban Resilience, carried by the wave wipe and closing on membership.

Bumper

6 s sign-off cut from the anthem's ending. Use it for pre-roll, event screens and the end of partner videos.
