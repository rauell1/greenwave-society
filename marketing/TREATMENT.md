# Treatment

## The Wave (30 s anthem)

**Logline.** One young person becomes a movement: a single gold seed lands, and the line
it draws grows into a crest of people, hills of trees, the lights of 25 communities, and
finally folds into the Greenwave emblem.

**Structures considered.**
1. A day in the life of one member, from morning to a community event. Too literal for
   30 s and needs photography the kit could not reach.
2. A countdown of the impact numbers. Clear but reads like an annual report.
3. **One line that keeps becoming something bigger** (chosen). It gives the film a single
   continuous object, puts the numbers inside the image instead of on top of it, and ends
   by turning the landscape into the real logo, which only code animation can do cleanly.

**Arc.** Setup: the seed and "It starts with one." Turn: the silence before the drop,
then *Lead. Build. Connect.* on three beats. Ending: the land closes into the half-globe,
and the opening seed returns as the full stop of the wordmark.

**Beat sheet (96 BPM, bar = 2.5 s).**

| Time | Bar | Picture | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–2.5 | 1 | Night. Gold seed falls, lands at 0.9 s, the lime line shoots to both edges. *It starts with one.* | Kalimba D5 + low marimba on the landing |
| 2.5–7.5 | 2–3 | Camera pulls back; the line undulates and young people pop up along it. Counter to **500+** | Ostinato wakes, shaker, bass; a tiny pluck per person |
| 7.5–12.5 | 4–5 | Dawn. The wave rises into hills, acacias grow, sun rises; slow pan. Counter to **10,000** | Full groove lands; marimba + rustle per tree |
| 12.5–15.0 | 6 | Dusk breath. 25 lights appear on the hills. *Across 25 communities.* | Pad and rising plinks, then **silence** 14.35–15.0 |
| 15.0–20.0 | 7–8 | Golden day. *Lead.* (a figure steps up, gold ring) *Build.* (skyline rises) *Connect.* (lights link up) | Drop: boom + crash on each word, marimba blocks |
| 20.0–25.0 | 9–10 | Signature move: the ground band closes into the half-globe, continents surface, turbine rises and spins. *Together, we are the wave.* | Whoosh, thinned groove, riser, **silence** 24.55–25.0 |
| 25.0–30.0 | 11–12 | Hit. Emblem slides into the lockup; the gold seed drops in as the full stop; tagline, *Join the wave*, greenwavesociety.org | Hit chord, the seed motif returns, chime on the call to action |

**Camera.** Pull-back reveal (0–7.5), lateral drift with parallax hills (7.5–12.5), hold
(12.5–15), slow push (15–20), pull and re-centre into the emblem (20–22.5), slide into the
lockup (25–26.2).

**Per-shape staging.** 16:9 keeps the type in a left column with the world shifted right;
9:16 and 4:5 stack type above the action and lift the horizon; 1:1 uses a single-line
wordmark under a smaller emblem.

## Six Ways In (20 s programme reel)

**Logline.** Six programmes, one beat each, carried card to card by the house wave wipe.

Intro bar: *Six ways to join the wave.* Bars 2–7: The 50 Percent, Youth Pulse, Climate
Action, Conservation, Leadership, Urban Resilience, each with its own colour, an animated
icon drawn for that programme, and a progress bar. Bar 8: the emblem builds, wordmark,
"Find your programme. Membership is open." and the call to action.

## Sonic identity

Kalimba and marimba (East African thumb piano and its wooden cousin), shaker, warm bass,
D major over D · Bm · G · A. Both films share it, so the bumper, the reel and the anthem
sound like one campaign.
